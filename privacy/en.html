PicBinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Summary

PicBinder does not collect personal data. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your photos and texts stay on your phone and, if you turn on backup, in your own Google Drive. The developer receives none of them.

Who is responsible

PicBinder is developed by Alexandre Ferreira, in Brazil. Contact: picbinder.app@gmail.com

What the app uses and why

  • Camera: to take photos, which are saved in the folder you choose on your phone.
  • Photo folder: you choose a folder with the Android folder picker; the app accesses only that folder.
  • Location (optional): with the pin turned on, GPS coordinates are written inside the photo (EXIF) and, if you want, in a .gpx file next to it. They are not sent to the developer. Note: anyone who receives the original photo file can see where it was taken.
  • Texts and notes: saved in a .txt file next to the photo, on your phone.
  • OCR (text recognition): done on the phone itself by ML Kit, a Google library. The image and the recognized text never leave the device. ML Kit sends Google technical performance and usage metrics about the feature (not the content of your photos), under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): when you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account and grant access. PicBinder uses Drive's most restricted permission: it can only see the files it created itself. Photos, .txt and .gpx files go straight from your phone to the PicBinder folder in your Drive; the developer has no access to them. You can turn backup off at any time in Settings and remove the authorization at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Notifications: used only to show backup progress.

Purchases (PicBinder Pro)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. Payment is made to Google; the developer does not receive your card details. Google gives the developer order details (such as order number, product, date and country), used only for support and tax obligations.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We have no server of our own: nothing you do in the app goes through the developer.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device and, if you use backup, in your Google Drive, under your control. Uninstalling the app deletes its settings and button list. Photos and files in your folder and in Drive remain, and you can delete them whenever you want.

Your rights (GDPR and LGPD)

Since the developer keeps none of your data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. For any question, write to picbinder.app@gmail.com. For data processed by Google (account, Drive, Google Play, ML Kit), see https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

PicBinder is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
